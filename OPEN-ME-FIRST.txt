WOMEN'S VOICES — COMPLETE PROJECT
=================================

This folder contains the complete website and all pages.

TO VIEW IT:
1. Right-click START-WEBSITE.command
2. Choose Open
3. If macOS asks again, click Open

The script always runs this exact folder and opens:
http://localhost:3000

TO UPLOAD IT TO GITHUB:
1. Stop the website with Control+C in Terminal
2. Right-click UPLOAD-TO-GITHUB.command
3. Choose Open

Correct live deployment:
https://womenvoices4.vercel.app

Do not use https://womenvoices2.vercel.app — that is the old website.
